DIY Buying Planner

About this site

DIY Buying Planner is a small collection of free UK-focused tools designed to turn project measurements into clearer material and pack-buying decisions.

What the tools do

The calculators separate straightforward maths from product or construction assumptions. Where a reliable conversion depends on a supplier's stated coverage, yield or pack size, the tool asks you to enter that information rather than inventing a density or product performance figure.

The current tools cover patio materials planning, general whole-pack optimisation and flooring pack calculations. Calculations run in your browser and do not require an account.

How to use the results

Use the outputs as planning estimates and always verify installation requirements, product suitability, stated coverage, waste allowance and pack details against the relevant manufacturer or supplier before buying.

The site may later use advertising or clearly disclosed affiliate links to fund continued development. Commercial relationships will not change the calculation logic or turn paid placements into technical recommendations.

Corrections and development

The site is being developed iteratively. If a calculator result looks wrong, do not rely on it for a purchase until the inputs and product information have been checked. New tools are added only where they can provide a useful buying or planning function rather than duplicating existing pages.
