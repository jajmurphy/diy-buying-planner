    How the calculation works

    Measured area is the sum of room length × width. Required coverage is measured area × (1 + waste allowance). Whole packs are then rounded up. Each flooring product is assessed independently using whole packs. Where prices are supplied, the calculator selects the lowest-cost complete-product option. Different flooring products are never mixed into one recommended floor basket.
