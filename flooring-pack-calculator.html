    Before buying: check the exact pack coverage, batch/dye-lot requirements, installation pattern and waste recommendation for your chosen flooring. This calculator optimises pack quantities; it does not determine whether a product is suitable for the room.

    How the calculation works

    Measured area is the sum of each room length × width. Required coverage is measured area × (1 + waste allowance). The optimiser then searches whole-number combinations of the pack options you entered and selects the lowest-cost basket that reaches or exceeds the required coverage.

    If two baskets cost the same, the calculator prefers the one with less surplus, then fewer total packs. Blank or invalid pack rows are ignored.

    Why compare pack options?

    A single flooring product may be sold in one fixed pack size, but when comparing several products or pack formats, the cheapest price per square metre does not always create the cheapest final basket. Whole-pack rounding can change the outcome. This tool compares the actual packs you can buy rather than rounding each option separately.
