How the calculation works

Area = length × width. Paving target = area × (1 + waste allowance). Sub-base volume = area × compacted depth. Mortar volume = area × bed depth.

Paving optimisation searches integer combinations of the entered products and returns the lowest-cost combination that meets or exceeds the paving target. If a product price is blank, the tool can still calculate the number of packs required for that product.

Construction basis and limitations

Manufacturer guidance varies by paving system. The defaults are editable rather than presented as universal rules.
