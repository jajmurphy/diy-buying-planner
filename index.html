How the calculation works

Area = length × width. Paving target = area × (1 + waste allowance). Sub-base volume = area × compacted depth. Mortar volume = area × bed depth.

Each paving product is evaluated on its own. The planner rounds that product up to whole packs, then compares complete-product options by total cost where prices are available. It never mixes different paving products into one recommended patio basket. If prices are blank, it prefers the option with the least surplus, then fewer packs.

Construction basis and limitations

Manufacturer guidance varies by paving system. The defaults are editable rather than presented as universal rules.
