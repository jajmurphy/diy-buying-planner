How the calculation works

Area = length × width. Paving target = area × (1 + waste allowance). Sub-base volume = area × compacted depth. Mortar volume = area × bed depth. The mortar split is shown by volume using the selected sand:cement ratio.

Paving optimisation searches integer combinations of the entered packs and returns the lowest-cost combination that meets or exceeds the paving target. Ties are resolved in favour of lower surplus, then fewer packs.

Construction basis and limitations

Current UK manufacturer guidance varies by paving system. Marshalls' general patio guide describes a 100 mm compacted sub-base, about 40 mm concrete/mortar layer and a 3:1 sand:cement mix. Bradstone's concrete/natural-stone guidance describes a 100 mm sub-base, around 30 mm mortar bed and a 4:1 sharp-sand:cement mix. Bradstone also advises a fall and manufacturer-specific joint widths/priming. The defaults in this prototype are therefore editable rather than presented as universal rules.

Sources: Marshalls — How to Build and Lay a Patio; Bradstone — How to Lay Concrete Paving; Tarmac — MOT Type 1.
