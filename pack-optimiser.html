    How it works

    The calculator searches whole-number combinations of the packs you enter. A valid basket must cover at least your required quantity. It chooses the lowest total cost; if two baskets cost the same, it prefers the one with less surplus, then the one using fewer packs.

    This is purchase optimisation, not a construction recommendation. Add any waste allowance to your required quantity before using the optimiser if your project needs one.

    Example

    If you need 43 m² and a pack covers 2.4 m², a single-pack calculation gives 18 packs, covering 43.2 m². With several pack sizes and prices, the optimiser checks whether a mixed combination is cheaper.
