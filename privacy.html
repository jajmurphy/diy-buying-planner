Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Calculator data

The calculators run in your browser. Measurements, pack sizes, prices and other values you enter are used by the page to produce results and are not intentionally submitted to a DIY Buying Planner database.

Website analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page views and website performance. Cloudflare describes Web Analytics as privacy-first and cookie-free and states that it does not collect or use visitors' personal data.

Advertising

Display advertising is not currently active. The site is being prepared to use Google AdSense. Before Google advertising is enabled, the required Google consent-management controls for visitors in the UK, EEA and Switzerland will be configured. Google advertising may use cookies, local storage and data processing according to the choices presented in that consent flow.

This policy will be updated when advertising becomes active and will identify the relevant advertising service and choices available to visitors.

Affiliate links

The site does not currently rely on affiliate links. If affiliate links are introduced, qualifying links and commercial relationships will be clearly disclosed.

Hosting and third-party services

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Requests to load the site and third-party resources are necessarily handled by the relevant service providers under their own privacy terms.

Changes

This policy may change as the site adds analytics, advertising, affiliate relationships or other features. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.
